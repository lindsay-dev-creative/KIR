Add it to Apple Mail

  1. If you added a Kirby Lane signature before, select it in Mail's Signatures tab and click the − button to delete it first. Then click Copy signature above.
  2. Open Mail. In the menu bar, choose Mail then Settings, and click the Signatures tab.
  3. In the left column, select the email account you want it on. Click the + button under the middle column to add a new signature, and name it Kirby Lane.
  4. Uncheck Always match my default message font under the right column. This keeps the signature's fonts and colors.
  5. Click into the right column, select any text already there, and paste with Command V.
  6. At the bottom, set Choose Signature to Kirby Lane so it's added to every new email.
  7. Close Settings. Start a new email and send a test to yourself to check it.

If the logo doesn't show up after pasting, click and drag across the whole signature above to select it, press Command C, and paste again. The logo can take a second to appear in Mail.

On your iPhone

The iPhone keeps its own signature. Open Settings, then Apps, Mail, Signature. You can type a short version there, like your name, title and phone number.
